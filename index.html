Where the numbers come from

IMDb and Rotten Tomatoes don't publish public APIs, so OMDb is used as the relay for both — it carries the IMDb score and vote count, the Rotten Tomatoes tomatometer and the Metascore. RT publishes a score per series (and per film), never per episode, so the episode heatmap uses IMDb episode scores, with TMDB's own episode averages alongside as a second opinion.
